## How to study each note

For every idea, ask:
1. What does the term mean in plain language?
2. What is happening at the particle level?
3. Why does it happen?
4. Which equation or model describes it?
5. What assumptions and limits does the model have?
6. Can I explain it in my own words and solve a new example?

## Questions to revisit

- What does an electron probability distribution actually represent?
- Why can a combined quantum arrangement make a bond stable?
- How do we compare the energy of a whole reaction rather than one bond?
- How can a thermodynamically favorable reaction be slow?
- How does atomic structure lead to the properties we can observe?
