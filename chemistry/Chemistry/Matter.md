# Matter

## What does matter mean?
In introductory chemistry, **matter** is physical stuff that has mass and occupies space. A rock, water, and air are all matter. Air can be hard to notice, but it still has mass and takes up space.

**Mass** tells us how much matter an object contains. It is different from **weight**, which is the force of gravity acting on an object.

## What is matter made of?
Ordinary chemical substances are built from [[Atoms]] or from structures atoms form, such as molecules and charged particles called ions.

Atoms are not tiny solid beads. Each has a very small, dense [[Nucleus]] and a surrounding region where [[Electron]]s can be found.

## Why does this matter?
Chemistry connects the invisible behavior of particles to visible properties: whether a material is hard, flows, conducts electricity, dissolves, or reacts.
