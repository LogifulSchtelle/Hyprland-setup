# The Nucleus

## What is the nucleus?
The **nucleus** is the tiny, dense center of an atom. It contains [[Proton]]s and, in most atoms, [[Neutron]]s. It contains almost all the atom's mass, even though it occupies only a very small part of the atom's overall size.

The [[Electron Cloud]] occupies the much larger region around the nucleus.

## What are protons and neutrons made of?
Protons and neutrons are not elementary particles. Each is made from smaller particles called **quarks**, held together by the **strong interaction**. This is a deeper layer of physics than we need to understand ordinary chemical bonding, but it explains why the nucleus itself has structure.

## Why don't protons simply fly apart?
Protons all have positive electric charge, so they electrically repel one another. At the very short distances inside a nucleus, the strong nuclear interaction can overcome that electric repulsion and bind the nuclear particles together.
