It is the region around the [Nucleus]() where [[Electron]]s can be found.
The electron cloud represents that distribution of probabilities.
when two atoms approach, their electrons can occupy a combined quantum arrangement. That changes the energy of the system and can allow a chemical bond to form.

Each hydrogen atom has one electron. When they form a hydrogen molecule, the two electrons occupy a shared molecular arrangement influenced by both nuclei.

This is called a covalent bond.

That arrangement can help hold the nuclei at a stable distance.
One subtle point: sharing electrons does not mean the electrons are stationary between the nuclei, nor does it mean each electron literally travels back and forth like a shuttle. The quantum description is more complicated, but we don't need the advanced mathematics yet.
