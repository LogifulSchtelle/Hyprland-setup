# Complete Chemistry Course

A linked, why-first course from first principles through core general chemistry. Read in numerical order, but follow glossary links whenever a term is unfamiliar.

## How to use this folder

1. Read a lesson slowly and explain its central idea in your own words.
2. Work examples on paper before reading their solutions.
3. Follow Obsidian links instead of memorizing an unfamiliar word in isolation.
4. Return to earlier notes when a later concept changes how you understand them.
5. Treat diagrams and analogies as models with limits, not literal photographs of atoms.

## What this course establishes

By the end, you should be able to explain the basic links between atomic structure, periodic patterns, chemical bonding, molecular shape, intermolecular forces, states of matter, reaction equations, amount calculations, energy, reaction rates, equilibrium, acids and bases, redox, organic structures, and nuclear change.

This is a substantial foundation, not the literal end of all chemistry. Advanced physical chemistry, spectroscopy, analytical chemistry, coordination chemistry, biochemistry, materials chemistry, and specialist research each extend these foundations.
