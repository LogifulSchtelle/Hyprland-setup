# Neutron

## What is a neutron?
A **neutron** is a particle with no net electric charge. Neutrons are found in the nucleus of most atoms. A common exception is ordinary hydrogen-1, whose nucleus contains just one proton.

Neutrons contribute to an atom's mass and affect the stability of its nucleus.

## What if the neutron count changes?
The element stays the same as long as the proton count stays the same. A different neutron count creates a different **isotope**, meaning a version of the same element with a different number of neutrons.

For example, carbon-12 and carbon-14 both have six protons but different numbers of neutrons.
