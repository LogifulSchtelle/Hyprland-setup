	Energy is a measurable property of phyiscal systems that help determine what changes can occur

##### Examples of Energy
==🔴Kinetic Energy== is associated with motion
==🟠Thermal Energy== is associated with microscopic motion and interactions among particles
==🟡Chemical Energy== is a useful shorthand for energy differences associated with chemical arrangements and reactions
==🟢Electrical Energy== is associated with electric charges and electric fields
==🔵Radiant Energy== is carried by electromagnetic radiation, including light
