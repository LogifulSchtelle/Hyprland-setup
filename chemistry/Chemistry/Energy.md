# Energy

## What is energy?
**Energy** is a measurable property of a physical system. It helps describe which changes can happen and how a system can transfer energy to its surroundings.

Energy is not a mysterious fuel hidden inside an object. It is a quantity we can track as things move, interact, heat up, emit light, or undergo chemical change.

## Useful forms of energy
- **Kinetic energy:** energy associated with motion.
- **Thermal energy:** energy associated with microscopic motion and interactions of particles.
- **Electrical energy:** energy related to electric charges and electric fields.
- **Radiant energy:** energy carried by electromagnetic radiation, including visible light.
- **Chemical energy:** a useful shorthand for energy differences associated with chemical arrangements and reactions.

Energy can be transferred or converted between forms.

## Energy and chemical bonds
When atoms form a stable bond, the combined system can end up at a lower energy than the separated atoms. The energy difference must be transferred elsewhere, for example as motion, heat, or radiation.

Separating bonded atoms requires energy to overcome the bond. For a complete reaction, compare the energy of **all** the starting and ending arrangements, not just one bond.
