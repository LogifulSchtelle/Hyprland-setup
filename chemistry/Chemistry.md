Before atoms, before molecules, before chemical reactions, we need three ideas:
- [[Matter]]
- [[Energy]]
- [[Interactions]]

The properties of a material depend not only on what its particles are, but also on how those particles are arranged and interact
	Example: Carbon is the same basic ingredient for both graphite in a pencil, and diamonds but the difference lies within the bonding and structure
	
Opposite electric charges attract. Like charges repel. [Proton]()s being positive, and [Electron]()s being negative.
