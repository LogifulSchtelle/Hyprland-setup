# Physics: From First Principles

Physics is the study of how the universe behaves: motion, forces, energy, matter, light, heat, space, time, and the rules connecting them. This vault is built **why-first**. The goal is not to memorize equations and hope they work; it is to understand what each quantity means, why a relationship follows, and when a model applies.

## The big branches

- **Mechanics:** motion, forces, momentum, rotation, gravity.
- **Waves and sound:** oscillations, wave motion, interference.
- **Thermal physics:** temperature, heat, internal energy, entropy.
- **Electricity and magnetism:** charge, fields, circuits, electromagnetic waves.
- **Optics:** reflection, refraction, lenses, images.
- **Modern physics:** relativity, quantum physics, atoms, nuclei, particles.
- **Applied physics:** materials, electronics, energy systems, astrophysics, medical imaging.

## The central idea

A physical theory is a compact, testable model of patterns in nature. A good model tells us what to expect, how uncertain that prediction is, and where the model stops working. Equations are not magic spells: they are precise statements about relationships between measurable quantities.
