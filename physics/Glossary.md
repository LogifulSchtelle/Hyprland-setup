# Physics Glossary

Plain-language definitions. Follow links to the lesson where a term is developed.

- **Acceleration:** rate at which velocity changes with time; it can mean speeding up, slowing down, or changing direction.
- **Accuracy:** closeness of a measurement to the accepted value.
- **Amplitude:** maximum displacement from an oscillation's equilibrium position.
- **Atom:** a nucleus surrounded by electrons; the basic unit retaining an element's chemical identity.
- **Average velocity:** displacement divided by elapsed time.
- **Buoyancy:** upward force a fluid exerts on an immersed object.
- **Charge:** a property that determines electromagnetic interactions; positive and negative signs describe two kinds.
- **Conservation law:** a rule stating that a quantity remains constant in an appropriately isolated system.
- **Displacement:** change in position, including direction.
- **Energy:** a conserved bookkeeping quantity associated with the ability of a system to change or cause change.
- **Entropy:** a state quantity connected to how energy and microscopic arrangements are distributed; it helps explain the direction of natural processes.
- **Force:** an interaction that changes motion or deforms an object; in Newtonian mechanics, net force equals mass times acceleration.
- **Frequency:** number of cycles per second, measured in hertz (Hz).
- **Field:** a value assigned throughout space, such as the electric or gravitational influence at each location.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposed explanation that can be tested.
- **Inertia:** resistance to changes in velocity; mass measures inertial response.
- **Kinetic energy:** energy associated with motion.
- **Mass:** measure of inertia; in everyday contexts also related to the amount of matter.
- **Model:** simplified representation of a physical system, with stated assumptions.
- **Momentum:** mass multiplied by velocity in classical mechanics.
- **Power:** rate of energy transfer or work, measured in watts.
- **Precision:** repeatability or fineness of measurement, not necessarily closeness to the truth.
- **Pressure:** force distributed over area, measured in pascals.
- **Scalar:** quantity described by magnitude alone.
- **Theory:** a broad, evidence-supported explanatory framework, not merely a guess.
- **Vector:** quantity with magnitude and direction.
- **Velocity:** rate of change of position, including direction.
- **Voltage:** energy transferred per unit charge between two points.
- **Wave:** a traveling disturbance or pattern that transports energy and momentum; matter need not travel along with the wave overall.
- **Work:** energy transferred by a force acting through displacement.
