## How to study each lesson

1. Read once for the story of the idea.
2. Write down each new quantity, its unit, and what it physically means.
3. Re-derive the main equation from the explanation where possible.
4. Work through the example with the solution covered.
5. Explain why a tempting wrong answer is wrong.
6. Return later and solve the checkpoint questions from memory.

## Questions to keep revisiting

- What exactly is being measured?
- What assumptions make this model valid?
- Which quantities are vectors (have direction) and which are scalars (only magnitude)?
- What is the system, and what lies outside it?
- Can units or limiting cases reveal an error?
- What observation could prove the explanation wrong?
